How Do Loans Work?

Small business loan process start to finish.


  • 1 | Make sure a SBA loan is right for your business.

    Requirements include:

    Your business first must be turned down for private financing

    Your business needs to meet the SBA's size requirements, which is generally businesses with 500 or less employees

  • 2 | Ensure your business is eligible.

    Requirements include:

    680+ FICO score for all primary business owners

    You’ll need 10% down payment

    2+ years experience in business

    Your current business is profitable

    No delinquencies or defaults on debt obligations to the U.S. government

  • 3 | You qualify? Great! Now find a lender.

    The SBA works with lenders to provide loans to small businesses. The agency doesn’t lend money directly to small business owners. You will need to find a potential lender.

    Resources to find a lender:

    Most active lenders found here

    Preferred banking lender qualifications found here

  • 4 | Identify the loan that works best for you.

    The SBA sets guidelines for loans made by its partnering lenders, community development organizations, and micro-lending institutions. The SBA guarantees these loans will be repaid, which eliminates some risk for the lending partners. Finding the right loan type is essential; work with your loan broker to make sure you're considering the right program.

    Resources to help evaluate loan types:

    Most common loan is the 7(a) loan

    List of the different SBA loan programs for specific purposes

    Loan rates calculator and current interest rates

  • 5 | Get all your paperwork together.

    Once you select a loan that works for you and ensured your eligibility; it’s time to get your paperwork together.

    Resources to gather documents:

    Checklist of documents needed

    Resources to evaluate credit ratings

    Questions to determine financial need

  • 6 | Apply to your loan.

    You’re all set! Finalize the loan application with your lender, in conjunction with the required application SBA paperwork. You may expect your loan application to take 2-3 months processing.

  • 7 | Success! You are approved for your loan!

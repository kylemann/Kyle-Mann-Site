Project submission: desktop and mobile

In approaching the press release page re-design, I first evaluated problems with the existing page: 1) repetitive headers/hierarchy h1 of “Press Room Current Information About Homeday” and h1 of “press releases”, and then “press release” labeling again over the documents 2) header imagery does not offer value and/or correlate with press releases 3) press contact and imagery section is buried below the data 4) the press release layout, four per row, is not an intuitive hierarchy.

My design aims to simplify the page hierarchy and display all articles of options (press releases, press company contact, company imagery assets) visible upon landing on the page. The press releases are stacked on each other starting with the most recent.

My further recommendations would be to make the press releases link to a HTML version page, allowing the user a seamless experience clicking in and out of the various releases, without having to download (keep as secondary option).

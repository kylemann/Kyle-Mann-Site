Latest Past Resume

Leading product design at Sentry for Codecov

I joined Codecov as its founding designer. During my time there, the product grew to 1M active users and $5M ARR and was acquired by Sentry.

My work spanned the core developer experience, product strategy, research, design systems, and how we worked as a product and design team. After the acquisition, I joined the broader Sentry design organization and continued leading Codecov's product design as its scope expanded beyond code coverage into testing, code quality, and the broader pre-release developer workflow.

My role grew with it: Senior Product Designer in October 2022, Design Lead in January 2024, and Product Design Manager in September 2024.
